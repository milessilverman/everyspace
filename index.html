How much more could your property be earning?

Enter your address and we will look up your parcel in public records and show a planning range for each way it could earn. Free, no sign-up.

Suggestions come from Los Angeles County's public address service and cover Los Angeles County only. They appear after you type 5 characters, and what you have typed so far is sent to that service to get them. No property lookup happens until you press the button.

When you press the button, your address is sent to Los Angeles County's public address, parcel and building-outline services. Only if those cannot answer (for example, the address is outside Los Angeles County) is it sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Nothing you enter is saved.

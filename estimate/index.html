Start typing and pick your address

Nothing you enter here is saved. While you type an address, the text is sent to Los Angeles County's public address service to suggest matches. When you press "Find my property", the address is sent to Los Angeles County's public map services to look up the property; addresses they cannot find are sent to OpenStreetMap's public search, and the property's map location is sent to OpenStreetMap to check for a pool and nearby transit. The map location may also be sent to US government map services to identify the county, and building outlines may be read from Overture Maps' public files. For addresses outside Los Angeles County, the text you type is also sent to Photon (an OpenStreetMap address search) to suggest matches, and the county's own public parcel service may be asked for the lot.

Property data: Los Angeles County public GIS services (address locator, assessor parcels, LARIAC building outlines). Some building outlines: Overture Maps Foundation. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Address suggestions © OpenStreetMap contributors (via Photon).

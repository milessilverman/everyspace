What could your property earn?

Type your address and we will look up your parcel in public records, list the ways the property could earn, and show a range for each. Free, no sign-up. Nothing you enter here is saved.

Suggestions come from Los Angeles County's public address service and cover Los Angeles County only. They appear after you type 5 characters, and what you have typed so far is sent to that service to get them.

We search only when you press the button. Your address is sent first to Los Angeles County's public address, parcel and building-outline services. Only if those cannot answer (for example, the address is outside Los Angeles County) is it sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim, and then, for the parcel, to that county's public parcel service. The property's map location is then sent to OpenStreetMap's Overpass service to find pools and transit stops (on the older path, buildings too).

Address search and suggestions, parcel records and building outlines: Los Angeles County public services (address locator, assessor parcel layer, LARIAC). Outside Los Angeles County: address search © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL) via Nominatim, with parcel data from the county assessor's public service. Pools and transit stops: © OpenStreetMap contributors.
